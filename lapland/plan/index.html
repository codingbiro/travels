How it works

Each activity has one of three tags:

MUST

The fixed points: book them or keep the time free.

Depends on the weather or snow: decide that morning.

Only with spare time or bad weather.

Good to know

  1. Monday and Tuesday are interchangeable. Siida is indoors, so put it on the greyer day.
  2. Sunday is an outdoor day, because Siida and Laanilan Kievari are closed on Sundays.
  3. Every evening: check the cloud forecast, sauna, then watch the sky.
  4. No more than an hour's drive each way. Inari and Siida, about 1 h away, are the farthest stops.

Choices to make

Decide before you book

These three drive what you book, so settle them first. The reindeer visit is the only one that can take you beyond an hour's drive.

Reindeer visit

Options

A. A Sámi herding family near Inari on Monday afternoon: Angeli (private, 2 h, from €100), Renniina (home visit, 2 h, from €60) or Inari Reindeer Farm.

B. Lapland Safaris' farm visit from Saariselkä on Tuesday (1.5 h).

Trade-off

A means real time with Sámi herders, but the drive home is about 1 h 5 – 1 h 15 (more than an hour) and it replaces Monday's lake afternoon. B is 5 min away and keeps Monday free; its listing doesn't describe the hosts as Sámi.

Default

A with Angeli if the address they give is within about an hour of the cabin; otherwise B.

Which night for the local-food dinner

Options

Laanilan Kievari on Monday or Tuesday (closed Sundays).

Trade-off

Monday makes one full local-food day with Sarrit's lunch. Tuesday is the darkest night, and dinner 18:30–20:30 eats into its best dark hours.

Default

Monday 18:30, five people; book now.

Huskies

Options

Kennel visit (about 1 h, runs without snow) or the 10 km self-drive sled safari (2.5 h, needs snow).

Trade-off

The kennel visit is certain. The safari is the bigger experience, but snow may not be down by 3 Nov.

Default

Book the kennel visit; ask Husky & Co to tell you if sled runs start before your date.

Decide the week before

These depend on the forecast.

Day order

Options

Keep Mon = Inari, Tue = huskies, or swap them on the forecast.

Trade-off

Swapping puts indoor Siida on the greyer day and Kaunispää's sunset on the clearer one, but the husky and reindeer bookings must be movable. Tuesday has the best aurora window either way.

Default

Keep the order; swap only if the forecast on Sat 31 Oct shows Monday clearly brighter.

Arrival-night shopping

Options

Kuukkeli in Saariselkä, 5 min from the cabin, or K‑Supermarket Ivalo, 10 km the other way from the airport.

Trade-off

Kuukkeli is on your way and enough for breakfast and an easy dinner. Ivalo has the fish and meat counter but adds about 20 min. Both may keep holiday hours on All Saints' Day.

Default

Kuukkeli on arrival; the big shop with fish at K‑Supermarket Ivalo on Monday's drive home. If you land late, bring breakfast basics.

Sauna extras

Options

Home sauna only, add Laanilan Kievari's saunas (outdoor hot tub, ice hole) with Monday's dinner, or the Kuurakaltio smoke sauna at Kiilopää.

Trade-off

Home is free and private. Laanilan is 1 km away and pairs with dinner. The smoke sauna is the most traditional; its public sessions are listed Mon–Sat, so not on Sunday's Kiilopää day.

Default

Home only.

Decide on the day

These are weather calls; the plan already holds both options.

Sunday hike

Options

Kiilopää summit (546 m, about 2 km each way, stairs) or the forest loops from Saariselkä (Iisakkipää 3 km, lit Aurorapolku 2.1 km).

Trade-off

The summit gives the big fell view but is pointless in cloud and hard in strong wind; the loops stay sheltered among trees.

Default

Summit if the top is out of the cloud at 09:30; otherwise the loops.

Monday afternoon

Options

Juutua rapids and Lake Inari shore, Sajos (weekdays; Duodji Shop), or Tuulispää's lean-to with a lake view, or Otsamo from Rovajärvi (418 m, 3 km each way).

Trade-off

Juutua and the shore are lake and river scenery; Sajos is indoor Sámi culture. Tuulispää's parking isn't ploughed until mid-February, so it depends on the gravel road. Otsamo is the best lake view of the trip, but more than an hour away and only on snow-free ground.

Default

Juutua and the shore; Sajos if it's sleeting; Tuulispää only if the road is clear and there's light left. Skip all of them if you chose the Sámi farm.

Aurora nights

Options

Stay at the cabin or drive to a clear patch (Kaunispää 12 min, Kiilopää 15 min, Ukonjärvi 40 min).

Trade-off

Driving finds clear sky under patchy cloud but needs a sober driver on dark roads with reindeer.

Default

Stay home unless it's cloudy overhead and clear nearby. On Tuesday, the best night, drive up to 45 min. Agree tonight's driver at dinner.

The evening ritual

  1. 15:30

    Is anything coming?

    Check FMI's auroras and space weather page. Its magnetometers include Ivalo and Kevo; a sharp jump there means go outside.

  2. 18:00

    Where's the clear sky?

    Yr's cloud map and Windy's low, mid and high cloud layers. Clear overhead: stay home. Cloudy here but clear within a 45‑minute drive: go.

  3. 19:00–01:00

    Keep looking out

    Step out every 15–20 min between sauna sessions; most displays fall between about 21:00 and 01:00. A faint grey arc low in the north often shows green on camera before your eyes see colour.

Sámi culture

Inari municipality, including Saariselkä and Ivalo, lies in the Sámi homeland, and Inari village is its cultural centre in Finland.

Within an hour's drive, Siida and Sajos in Inari are the easiest way in; a visit to a Sámi herding family near Inari goes deeper, but it is just over an hour away.

MUST

Siida · Monday

The Sámi Museum, European Museum of the Year 2024; since 2025 its exhibitions are run by the Sámi Museum alone. The main exhibition links Sámi culture with northern nature.

EXTRA

Sajos · Monday

The Sámi Parliament's building and cultural centre next to Siida, open weekdays. Its Duodji Shop sells Sámi-made handicraft.

Juutua trail

The only nature trail in the area with signs in Inari Sámi.

Three languages

Inari Sámi, North Sámi and Skolt Sámi are all spoken here; Siida's website runs in all three. Welcome, in Inari Sámi: tiervâpuáttim.

How to be a good guest

  • Ask before photographing people, homes or herders at work.
  • Buy handicraft with the Sámi Duodji label; it guarantees a Sámi maker.
  • Skip costumes and souvenirs that imitate the gákti, the Sámi dress.
  • Every reindeer has an owner. Don't chase, feed or call free-roaming reindeer.

Sauna

Sauna every night at the cabin, and every cool-off doubles as a sky check.

  1. Heat. Switch on 45–60 min ahead; aim for 70–80 °C.
  2. Löyly. A ladle of water on the stones; rounds of 10–15 min. Lower bench for first-timers.
  3. Cool off outside. Towel, boots, look north. Roll in the snow if there is any.
  4. Repeat two or three times. Water between rounds; nothing stronger if anyone may still drive to an aurora spot.

Weather and safety

  • November averages about −3 °C by day and −8 °C at night and is mostly overcast: cloud, not aurora, is the real gamble.
  • Snow is likely but not guaranteed in early November.
  • New lake ice is not safe. Stay off frozen lakes and rivers, however solid they look.
  • Parts of the national park have no mobile signal. Emergency number: 112.

Photography

The 70–350 works the days, the 12 mm the nights.

The sun never climbs above about 7°, so every clear daylight hour is low, raking light — about six and a half of them a day. The Sony 70–350 G works the days (105–525 mm equivalent on the a6100), the Samyang 12 mm f/2 the nights. The a6100 has no in-body stabilisation, so the lens's OSS does all the steadying.

Shooting in the cold

  • NP‑FW50 batteries drain fast in the cold, and OSS plus continuous AF use more. Carry three or more in an inside pocket and rotate.
  • Before going indoors, seal the camera in a zip bag and let it warm up before opening it, so no condensation forms inside.
  • Lens hood on all day: with the sun at 7°, it's always close to the frame.
  • Phones: night mode catches aurora well if braced on something solid.
